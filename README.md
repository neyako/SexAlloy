# SexAlloy

SexAlloy is an LSPosed module that removes ads and adds extra features to popular Android apps on a rooted phone, without modifying the apps themselves.

This repository is the maintained continuation of [SexAlloy](https://github.com/gnadgnaoh/SexAlloy) (originally forked from [NexAlloy](https://github.com/NexAlloy/NexAlloy)), keeping extra apps working on newer versions while staying up to date with upstream.

## What you need

- A rooted Android phone with a recent version of LSPosed.
- This is not an official Morphe or ReVanced project. Please do not ask their developers for support.

## Download

- **Stable release**: [Latest release](https://github.com/neyako/SexAlloy/releases/latest)
- **Dev build**: [Autobuild](https://github.com/neyako/SexAlloy/releases/tag/autobuild)

A dev build is published on every change, and a stable release is cut automatically every few changes.

## Install

1. Install the APK. It shows up on your phone as **NexAlloy**.
2. Open LSPosed, enable NexAlloy, and tick the apps you want to use it with.
3. Force stop and reopen each ticked app.
4. Open the NexAlloy app to turn individual features on or off for each app. For YouTube and YouTube Music, extra options also appear inside each app's own Settings menu.

## Supported apps and features

Features marked *(off by default)* can be enabled in the NexAlloy app.

- Remove general ads, video ads, and Premium promotions
- SponsorBlock (skip sponsors and other unwanted segments)
- Return YouTube Dislike (dislike counts, plus like counts and ratios on feed thumbnails)
- DeArrow (community titles and thumbnails, or video frame thumbnails)
- Background playback, including for kids videos
- AI channel filter and reporting (AiSList)
- Add to queue
- Swipe controls for volume, brightness, playback speed, and seeking
- Seekbar options (tap to seek, slide to seek, thumbnail previews, hide seekbar, live stream seeking and extended rewind)
- Remove tracking from shared links
- Always open the Android system share sheet
- Hide and customize navigation bar buttons and toolbar
- Remember default video quality, show quality button, and use the advanced quality menu
- Video player button to copy video links
- Copy video titles and comments by long-pressing
- Download videos in-app or open an external downloader app
- Download images and GIFs from community posts
- Translate community posts
- Custom playback speeds, remember default speed, and speed button in player
- Hide layout components, Shorts components, feed menu items, and video action buttons
- Stop Shorts from resuming on app startup
- Always use the original audio track
- Disable or force HDR, and disable VP9 or AV1 video formats
- Stop captions from turning on automatically
- Fix missing avatars and channel images blocked in some regions
- Change start page and top-left header logo
- Search inside the current channel
- Whitelist specific channels for ads or playback speed
